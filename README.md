# Inventory & Stock Management System

## Project Overview

The Inventory & Stock Management System is a web-based application for recording and monitoring product inventory, suppliers, stock movements, and sales. It is designed to give authorized users a centralized view of available stock and help them respond quickly to low-stock situations.

## Problem Statement

Manual inventory records can make it difficult to maintain accurate stock levels, identify shortages, track supplier information, and keep a reliable history of stock-in and stock-out transactions. These gaps can lead to overstocking, stockouts, delayed restocking, and inaccurate sales records.

This project provides a structured system for maintaining product and supplier data, recording inventory movements, updating stock after sales, and presenting current inventory status through alerts and a dashboard.

## Project Objectives

- Provide secure access to the inventory system.
- Manage products and supplier records.
- Record stock-in and stock-out transactions.
- Deduct stock automatically when sales are recorded.
- Alert users when products reach low-stock levels.
- Present inventory activity and current status through a dashboard.
- Maintain project requirements, design documents, database resources, tests, and usage documentation in the repository.
